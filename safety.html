Safety by design

Safety is part of the lesson, not a warning added afterwards.

ATHAR is designed so children practise judgement, privacy and involving trusted adults while the product itself remains bounded.

Six commitments

01

No open child chatbot

Children work inside reviewed missions.

02

No advertising

No sponsored content or behavioural targeting.

03

No emotional companion

Hamdan and Hessa give thinking hints and step back.

04

No automated child profiling

No automated diagnosis or high-stakes AI grading.

05

Hints are recorded honestly

Help does not become false mastery.

06

Trusted adults stay in the loop

High-stakes situations route back to a trusted adult.

Why there is no unrestricted chatbot

The pilot teaches AI literacy through deterministic, reviewed interactions.

Why companions stay small

Hamdan and Hessa may point out a clue, then step back.

What is stored

The pilot stores bounded progress locally in the browser.

The safety loop

  • PreventBound the experience before a risky interaction begins.
  • DetectNotice unsafe choices and high-stakes situations.
  • RespondGive a clear safety nudge.
  • RecoverReturn to a safe next step.
  • AssureTest, review and record evidence honestly.

A safety behaviour children practise

Mission 6 includes a medicine-safety scenario that routes the child to a trusted adult.

What is not solved yet

This is still a pilot, and several production controls remain intentionally unfinished.
