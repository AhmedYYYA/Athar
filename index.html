What this will never do

Most of the work went into what we left out. These are commitments, and you should hold us to them.

  • Open a chatbot to a child Children work inside missions that were written and reviewed, never a blank box that answers anything.
  • Sell them anything No advertising, no sponsored content, no behaviour tracked to target them later.
  • Pretend to be their friend Hamdan and Hessa point something out and then get out of the way. They never ask for affection or secrecy.
  • Keep a secret from a trusted adult Nothing here encourages a child to hide what they are doing. The opposite is taught directly.
  • Score or profile a learner No automated diagnosis, no ranking, no behavioural profile built from how a child plays.
  • Call a hint mastery Taking help is fine and it is recorded as help. A teacher sees the difference.
