THE IDEA

AI literacy that strengthens the child, not dependence on the tool.

ATHAR is designed around one governing principle: the child remains the thinker. AI remains the tool.

01

Human agency

Children set the goal, make choices and remain accountable for the work.

02

Judgment before trust

Confidence, fluency and speed are never treated as proof of correctness.

03

Creation over copying

AI helps children develop and improve ideas rather than replace original effort.

04

Responsible impact

Learners consider consequences, fairness, privacy and the people affected.

CHILD-SAFE BY DESIGN

Safety is architecture, not a disclaimer.

ATHAR separates prevention, detection, response, recovery and assurance so child-facing AI practice remains bounded and supervised.
