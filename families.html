What ATHAR does — and does not do

Designed for
  • Short guided learning missions
  • Independent vs supported evidence
  • Safety and Skills Passports
  • Family conversations and trusted-adult involvement
Not designed for
  • Reading a child's raw private answers
  • An unrestricted child chatbot
  • Emotional dependency or secret keeping
  • Advertising or behavioural targeting
