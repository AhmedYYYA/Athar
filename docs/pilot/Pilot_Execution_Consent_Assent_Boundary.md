# ATHAR | أثر — Consent & Assent Boundary

The pilot protocol requires parent/guardian consent and child assent through the pilot operator's approved process before participation.

Stage 7 provides no consent database, signature capture or legal-form generator because the correct process depends on the actual operator, site, purpose and governance review.

Before launch, the operator must establish:
- who provides parent/guardian permission and how it is documented;
- how the child is given an age-appropriate explanation and asked for assent;
- how refusal/withdrawal is handled;
- who can verify completion before a session starts;
- where consent/assent records are stored and retained;
- whether additional institutional/research/privacy notices apply.

A child may stop a session even after prior assent. Facilitators must not pressure completion for pilot metrics.
