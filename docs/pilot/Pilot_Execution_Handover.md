# ATHAR | أثر — Stage 7 Handover

## For the Pilot Lead
Start with `Pilot_Execution_Index.md`, then complete `Pilot_Execution_Readiness_Checklist.md`. Do not schedule child sessions until governance dependencies and shakedown are closed.

## For facilitators
Use `Facilitator_Quick_Card.md` and the Arabic facilitator brief as applicable. The core rule is: support navigation and reflection without taking over the child's thinking.

## For technical staff
Record an immutable website SHA in `Pilot_Baseline_Record.md`. Treat S3 defects as pause conditions and rerun the established QA gate after relevant fixes.

## For evidence/data staff
Use the minimal CSV schemas and data dictionary. Keep names and private narratives out of routine analytical records.

## For safeguarding/privacy authority
ATHAR does not provide case management. Review the operator's external process and ensure the escalation route is known before launch.

## For decision authority
Use the success metrics, gap register, closeout checklist and Go/No-Go memo. A bounded pilot can justify a next phase; it cannot by itself establish production readiness.
