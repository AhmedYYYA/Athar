# ATHAR | أثر — Pilot Retrospective Template

Complete after the formal Go/No-Go decision.

## What worked
- product experience;
- Arabic/English delivery;
- age differentiation;
- facilitation;
- safeguarding/privacy controls;
- evidence interpretation;
- technical operations.

## What failed or created friction
Separate product, curriculum, process, governance and site/device issues.

## What should change before the next phase
Prioritize critical/high items and link them to the production gap register.

## What should remain locked
Record decisions that the pilot evidence supports retaining so later redesign does not discard validated strengths without reason.

## Evidence caveat
Retrospective opinions must not overwrite the formal pilot evidence or turn individual anecdotes into generalized findings.
