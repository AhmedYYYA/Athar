## 7. Decision rules
### GO
Use only when the observed evidence supports expansion and no critical safeguarding, privacy, accessibility or reliability issue remains open.

### CONDITIONAL GO
Use when educational value and safe use are supported, but named material gaps must close before scale.

### REWORK
Use when the concept remains promising but material UX, curriculum, evidence or operating-model issues make current findings insufficient for scale.

### NO-GO
Use when unresolved risk or lack of educational evidence makes continuation unjustified under the tested model.

## 8. Evidence integrity statement
Confirm that:
- observations were coded rather than inflated into precise claims unsupported by the pilot;
- routine analytical records did not require child names;
- raw private conversations were not treated as standard evidence;
- product defects and pedagogy findings were distinguished;
- any mid-pilot baseline change is identified and results before/after that change are not silently pooled.
