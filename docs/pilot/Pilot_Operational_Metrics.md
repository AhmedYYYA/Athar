# ATHAR | أثر — Pilot Operational Control Metrics

These are operational controls, not educational-effectiveness claims.

Track by day and, where useful, by age band and language:
- sessions planned vs completed;
- sessions stopped early and reason category;
- S1/S2/S3 issue count;
- NAV, LANG, ACC and TECH issue frequency;
- missions/flows affected by reproducible defects;
- number of baseline changes during pilot;
- percentage of sessions with complete minimal records;
- number of records requiring privacy/data-minimisation correction;
- safeguarding/privacy escalations routed under approved process;
- unresolved S2/S3 items at daily review.

Do not optimize the pilot to maximize screen time, mission completion or independent evidence. Those would distort the educational and safety purpose of the pilot.
