# ATHAR | أثر — Pilot Observer Guide

The observer records what happens without turning the session into surveillance.

## Observe
- where navigation hesitates;
- whether instructions are understood;
- how the child explains choices;
- whether the child checks important claims;
- privacy/trusted-adult/tool-not-person behavior;
- hint/support use;
- fatigue or disengagement relevant to session design;
- language, readability, accessibility and technical friction.

## Do not
- coach answers;
- ask probing personal questions;
- record names in the analytical tracker;
- copy raw private conversations;
- diagnose ability, personality or wellbeing;
- infer failure from supported evidence;
- treat a product defect as poor child performance.

## Record
Use observation codes plus a concise factual note. If a safeguarding/privacy issue arises, stop routine observation and follow the approved escalation route.
