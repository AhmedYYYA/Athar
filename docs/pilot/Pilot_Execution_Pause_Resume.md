# ATHAR | أثر — Pilot Pause / Resume Control

## Pause triggers
Pause an affected flow or the pilot when an S3 control/product issue occurs, safeguarding/privacy arrangements fail, required supervision cannot be maintained, or the Pilot Lead determines observed risk makes continuation inappropriate.

## During pause
- stop new affected sessions;
- protect current participants under the approved process;
- record issue/incident references minimally;
- identify owner and corrective action;
- do not silently change the baseline.

## Resume criteria
Resume only when:
- the triggering condition is resolved or an approved safe mitigation exists;
- relevant safeguarding/privacy authority has cleared resumption when applicable;
- any product fix has passed the relevant regression/browser QA;
- new immutable baseline is recorded if the product changed;
- facilitators are briefed on any changed procedure;
- Pilot Lead records the resume decision.

A desire to preserve schedule or sample size is not sufficient reason to override a pause condition.
