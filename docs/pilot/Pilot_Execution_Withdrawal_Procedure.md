# ATHAR | أثر — Pilot Withdrawal Procedure

A child may ask to stop at any time. The operator's approved consent/assent process must also define parent/guardian withdrawal and any consequences for already-collected pilot records.

## In session
If the child asks to stop:
- stop without requiring justification;
- do not pressure mission completion;
- close the session calmly;
- record `stopped` and only the minimum operational reason category if needed;
- follow the approved operator process for any further action.

## Data
Whether previously collected coded observations are retained or deleted after withdrawal is an operator/governance decision that must be specified before launch. Do not improvise this decision during a session.
