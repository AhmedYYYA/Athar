# ATHAR | أثر — Safeguarding Operational Boundary

ATHAR facilitators must recognize and route safeguarding concerns; they are not asked to investigate, diagnose or manage a case through the product.

When a concern arises:
1. prioritize immediate child safety under the operator's approved procedure;
2. stop the relevant ATHAR activity when appropriate;
3. avoid probing for more detail through the lesson/session;
4. contact the named safeguarding authority;
5. keep the ATHAR analytical record to minimal routing/control information;
6. store any required formal safeguarding record only in the operator's approved system;
7. decide whether the session/flow/pilot must pause.

The exact mandatory reporting and escalation obligations depend on the real pilot operator/site and must be confirmed before launch. Stage 7 does not invent those procedures.
