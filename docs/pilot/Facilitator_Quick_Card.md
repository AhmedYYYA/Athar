# ATHAR | أثر — Facilitator Quick Card

## Before
- Confirm approved participation process is complete.
- Use participant code, not the child's name, in routine analytical records.
- Check language, age band, device/browser and browser-local state.
- Know the safeguarding escalation contact.

## Say
**English:** “ATHAR is a learning tool, not a person. You do the thinking. You can ask for help or stop at any time.”

**العربية:** «أثر أداة للتعلّم، وليس شخصاً. أنت من يفكّر. يمكنك طلب المساعدة أو التوقف في أي وقت.»

## During
- Observe before helping.
- Help navigation; do not give the answer.
- Ask: “How do you know?” / «كيف عرفت؟»
- Hints are allowed; supported evidence is not failure.
- Never ask the child to share private information.
- Hamdan/Hessa are guides, not AI friends or secret keepers.

## Stop
Stop the relevant activity if the child asks to stop, shows material persistent distress/confusion/fatigue, begins unnecessary sensitive disclosure, a safeguarding concern appears, a defect prevents safe use, or approved supervision cannot be maintained.

## After
- Ask 1–2 short reflection questions.
- Record coded observations, not unnecessary verbatim content.
- Log technical defects separately.
- Escalate S3 conditions immediately.
