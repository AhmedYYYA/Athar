# ATHAR | أثر — Pilot Evidence Boundaries

## Appropriate evidence
- mission completion/partial/stopped status;
- independent/supported/not-yet evidence signals;
- structured observation codes;
- child explanation summarized at concept level without unnecessary personal detail;
- navigation/localization/accessibility/technical friction;
- facilitator and parent/educator structured feedback;
- reproducible defect evidence;
- safety-behaviour observations relevant to the learning objectives.

## Do not treat as routine pilot evidence
- names/contact details;
- health/family circumstances;
- raw private conversations;
- verbatim sensitive disclosures;
- generalized intelligence/personality labels;
- rankings between children;
- unsupported inference about emotional state, diagnosis or ability;
- screen time as a proxy for learning quality.

## Interpretation
A supported result means assistance was used. It does not establish inability. An independent result is evidence within the specific mission context; it does not prove general mastery beyond the pilot task.

The pilot is designed to support decisions about ATHAR's learning experience, not to profile individual children.
