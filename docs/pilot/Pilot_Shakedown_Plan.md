# ATHAR | أثر — Pre-Cohort Shakedown Plan

## Purpose
Validate the operating model and current locked product baseline before exposing the planned 40–60-child cohort to avoid discovering basic process or device defects at scale.

## Scope
Use a **small supervised shakedown** sufficient to exercise both age experiences and both languages where practical. This is an operational readiness activity, not a substitute for the controlled pilot or a basis for population-level claims.

## Must exercise
- Arabic and English entry/navigation.
- Ages 7–9 and 10–12.
- At least one mission from each of: Understand It, Check It, Protect Yourself and See Inside.
- Independent and hint-supported paths.
- Hamdan/Hessa/no-companion choice where practical.
- Family/educator evidence interpretation demonstration.
- Stop/escalation drill without using a real child disclosure.
- Defect logging and daily-control decision workflow.

## Device sample
Include representative phone, tablet and laptop/desktop where available, prioritizing the actual devices intended for the pilot site.

## Exit gate
Do not authorize the planned cohort until:
- no S3 issue remains open;
- every S2 issue has an explicit mitigation/owner;
- Arabic/English blocking defects are closed;
- facilitator behavior and escalation routing are understood;
- trackers contain no unnecessary identifying fields;
- any product fix has passed the established regression/browser QA gate;
- Pilot Lead records the proceed/mitigate/pause decision.

## Shakedown report
Record baseline, devices, flows exercised, issue IDs, mitigations, and readiness decision. Do not characterize the shakedown as evidence of educational effectiveness.
