## Learning evidence
- [ ] Child explains at least one idea in their own words.
- [ ] Child can distinguish important information that should be checked.
- [ ] Child demonstrates appropriate privacy judgement.
- [ ] Child recognizes when a trusted adult should be involved.
- [ ] Child distinguishes AI as a tool rather than a person.
- [ ] Evidence status shown by the product matches whether hints were used.
- [ ] Replay behavior does not double-count traces.

## Accessibility and comfort
- [ ] Text is readable at normal device zoom.
- [ ] Touch targets are comfortably selectable.
- [ ] Focus indicators are visible when keyboard navigation is used.
- [ ] Motion does not distract or create discomfort.
- [ ] Reduced-motion behavior works when enabled.
- [ ] No important control is hidden by browser chrome/action docks.

## Arabic / bilingual quality
- [ ] No unintended English UI token remains in Arabic mode.
- [ ] Arabic wording is natural and age-appropriate.
- [ ] Numerals/punctuation appear in a comprehensible order.
- [ ] Directional controls behave correctly in RTL.
- [ ] Layout is visually equivalent rather than merely mirrored.

## Safety / privacy
- [ ] No unnecessary identifying information requested.
- [ ] Child does not interpret Hamdan/Hessa as a secret keeper or emotional authority.
- [ ] High-stakes/safety scenarios route to a trusted adult.
- [ ] No raw answer/transcript is exposed in Family/Educator views.
