# ATHAR | أثر — Educator Pilot Briefing

## Educational purpose
ATHAR provides a 16-mission bilingual foundation across six tracks:
1. Understand It | افهمه
2. Ask It Well | أحسِن سؤاله
3. Check It | تحقّق منه
4. Protect Yourself | احمِ نفسك
5. Create With It | أبدع به
6. See Inside | انظر في داخله

The objective is not to teach children to depend on AI. It is to develop agency, judgement, verification, privacy, responsible creation and computational thinking around AI.

## Classroom rhythm
Use a simple four-part rhythm:
**Frame → Explore → Discuss → Interpret evidence**.

- **Frame:** connect the mission to prior knowledge and set one clear question.
- **Explore:** let learners complete the bounded mission without being given answers.
- **Discuss:** ask learners to explain how they know.
- **Interpret evidence:** use independent/supported evidence as a teaching signal rather than an opaque score.

## Evidence rules
Completion and evidence are separate. A learner who uses a hint may complete a mission with **supported** evidence. A replay without hints may later demonstrate **independent** evidence. Do not present supported evidence as failure or use it for high-stakes ranking.

## Teacher role
The teacher remains the professional decision-maker. ATHAR can structure practice and surface bounded evidence. It does not diagnose learners, make high-stakes decisions, replace safeguarding judgement or provide an official school record.

## Pilot observation priorities
Observe whether learners:
- understand the instruction without repeated adult translation;
- explain why an answer is trustworthy or uncertain;
- know when to verify information;
- protect personal information;
- identify when a trusted adult should be involved;
- distinguish AI as a tool rather than a person;
- retain their own contribution and credit sources/AI help appropriately;
- understand steps, conditions and loops at an age-appropriate level.

## Current system boundary
The pilot does not yet provide teacher-created cohorts, assignments, centralized progress, production identity/consent, LMS integration or official records. Any class list or participant mapping must be operated separately under the pilot's approved governance process.
