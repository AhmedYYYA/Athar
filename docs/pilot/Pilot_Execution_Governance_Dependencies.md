# ATHAR | أثر — Pilot Governance Dependencies

The repository can prepare execution controls but cannot satisfy the following simply through code/documentation. Before child sessions, the pilot operator/institution must determine and approve, as applicable:

- who is legally/operationally responsible for the pilot;
- participant recruitment and eligibility;
- parent/guardian consent and child assent;
- safeguarding lead, escalation channels and applicable mandatory reporting procedures;
- pilot site authorization;
- what pilot records are collected, lawful/approved basis, storage location, access, retention and deletion;
- whether any ethics/research review is required by the institution or intended use;
- device/network administration and shared-device handling;
- accessibility accommodations for actual participants;
- incident management outside the ATHAR product;
- final authority to authorize, pause, resume and close the pilot.

Stage 7 deliberately does not fabricate these approvals. `Pilot_Execution_Readiness_Checklist.md` treats them as prerequisites.
