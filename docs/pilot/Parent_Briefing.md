# ATHAR | أثر — Parent / Guardian Pilot Briefing

## What ATHAR is
ATHAR is a bilingual Arabic-English learning experience for children aged 7–12. It teaches children to understand AI, ask better questions, check important answers, protect privacy, create responsibly and use step-by-step computational thinking.

**Core principle:** the child remains the thinker. AI remains the tool.

## What your child will use in this pilot
The current pilot contains short, reviewed learning missions. It does not give the child an unrestricted chatbot or open internet access. Progress is stored locally in the browser and is represented as mission completion, traces, badges and Safety/Skills Passport evidence.

## Independent and supported evidence
- **Independent:** the child demonstrated a skill without using a hint.
- **Supported:** a hint helped the child demonstrate the skill.

Supported evidence is not failure. It tells us where another attempt or a short conversation may help build independence.

## Your role
You do not need to sit beside the child for every click. We do ask you to remain available and to reinforce these habits:
- AI is a tool, not a person.
- AI can be wrong; important information should be checked.
- Private information stays private.
- Important safety, wellbeing or personal decisions involve a trusted adult.
- A child should not keep secrets with AI.

After a mission, one useful question is: **“How did you know?” | «كيف عرفت؟»**

## Privacy boundary
The current product does not require a child name, email, address, password or conversation transcript. The pilot operator may use separate consent/participant records for the study; those records are outside the ATHAR website and should follow the operator's approved privacy process.

## When to stop
Please tell the facilitator and stop the activity if your child becomes distressed, wants to stop, begins sharing unnecessary sensitive information, or raises a safety/wellbeing matter requiring adult attention.

## What this pilot is not
This pilot is not a school grade, psychological assessment, diagnosis, behavioural profile or production school account system. It is an evaluation of learning, usability, safety and trust.
