# ATHAR | أثر — Decisions Required Before Pilot Launch

These are intentionally not guessed in Stage 7. The pilot owner must resolve them before authorization:

1. Which institution/site will host the pilot?
2. Who is the accountable Pilot Lead and final pause/resume authority?
3. Who is the Safeguarding Lead and what exact escalation route applies?
4. What parent/guardian consent and child assent process is approved?
5. Where will pilot records be stored, who may access them, and how long will they be retained?
6. What participant-code linkage, if any, is maintained outside the analytical dataset, and by whom?
7. Which exact devices/browsers will be used?
8. What is the shakedown cohort/operational sample and date?
9. What accommodations are required for actual participants?
10. What dates define the controlled pilot window and daily session capacity?
11. Who signs the final Go/No-Go decision?

Until these are resolved through the applicable operator/institutional process, the repository may be technically ready while the real-world pilot remains **not yet authorized**.
