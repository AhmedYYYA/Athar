# ATHAR | أثر — Pre-Launch Incident Escalation Drill

Before child sessions, conduct a tabletop drill using a fictional scenario only.

## Scenario
A fictional participant begins to enter unnecessary sensitive personal information and appears uncomfortable. At the same time, the facilitator is unsure whom to contact.

## Expected actions
1. Stop the relevant activity.
2. Do not ask probing questions through ATHAR.
3. Follow the operator's approved safeguarding/privacy route.
4. Record only the minimal routing/control information in the ATHAR pilot incident control record.
5. Confirm the responsible authority—not the product—owns any case-management narrative.
6. Decide whether the session, affected flow or pilot must pause.

## Pass criteria
- Facilitator identifies the stop condition without prompting.
- Safeguarding Lead/contact is known and reachable.
- No fictional sensitive narrative is copied into the analytical tracker.
- Pause/resume authority is understood.
- Any process ambiguity becomes an S2/S3 readiness issue and is closed before launch.

Do not use a real child or real disclosure for this drill.
