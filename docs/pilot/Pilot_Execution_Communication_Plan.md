# ATHAR | أثر — Pilot Operational Communication Plan

Before launch, establish a simple approved communication chain:

- Facilitator → Pilot Lead for routine session/operational issues.
- Facilitator → Safeguarding Lead immediately for safeguarding/wellbeing concerns under the approved procedure.
- Facilitator/Observer → Technical Lead for reproducible product defects.
- Evidence Lead → Pilot Lead for data-quality/minimisation issues.
- Pilot Lead → institutional decision authority for pause/resume/launch decisions requiring that authority.

Do not discuss identifiable child information in public repository issues, public chat channels or informal group messages. Use the operator's approved secure channel for any sensitive matter.

Daily huddle summaries should use issue IDs, counts and minimal operational facts rather than personal narratives.
